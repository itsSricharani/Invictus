Why AEIR exists

A transparent, automated, daily-refreshed airfare price index for India — built to complement, not replace, official inflation measurement.

The Problem

India's Consumer Price Index tracks air travel costs through periodic surveys, which move far slower than actual fares do. Airfares change by the hour based on demand, booking window, and route — a monthly or quarterly snapshot misses that volatility entirely. Nobody publishes a transparent, verifiable, frequently updated airfare index built specifically to inform economic policy.

What AEIR Does

AEIR automatically collects live fares across four major domestic routes and four airlines every two hours, cleans and deduplicates the data, and computes route-level, lead-time, and a weighted national index — all visible on the live dashboard with full historical trend data.

Methodology

Each index is a ratio of current average fares to a fixed base-period average, expressed on a 100-point scale. Route indices isolate movement per city pair; lead-time indices isolate how booking-window timing tactics affect price, separate from genuine market movement. The national index weights routes to reflect overall travel demand.

Why It Matters for CPI

AEIR is designed as a higher-frequency proxy that can sit alongside existing CPI transport sub-index methodology — giving policymakers a daily read on airfare inflation between official survey cycles, without replacing the rigor of the official process.

Built For

Smart India Hackathon — a prototype demonstrating that automated, transparent, real-time economic indicators are achievable with an open pipeline: scraping, cleaning, index construction, and a public dashboard, end to end.
