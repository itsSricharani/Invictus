METHODOLOGY

How the Aeir index is built.

A transparent, open-pipeline approach to measuring domestic airfare movements in India — designed to complement official inflation methodology at higher frequency.

01

The Problem

India's Consumer Price Index tracks air travel costs through periodic surveys that move far slower than actual fares. Airfares change by the hour based on demand, booking window, and route — a monthly or quarterly snapshot misses that volatility entirely.

No public, verifiable, high-frequency airfare price index exists for domestic Indian aviation. Aeir is designed to fill that gap.

02

Data Collection

Aeir collects live fares automatically every 5 hours across the four highest-volume domestic routes in India, for multiple airlines and lead times (booking windows).

Each collection run generates up to 80 fare observations. New records are checked against existing data and deduplicated before storage.

04

Index Construction

Aeir uses a Laspeyres-style fixed-base price index. Each index expresses current fares as a ratio of fares in the base period, scaled to 100.

Route Index = (Avg Farecurrent / Avg Farebase) × 100
Computed separately for each route. A value above 100 means fares are higher than the base period; below 100 means they are lower.
National APIx = Σ (Route Indexi × Weighti)
Route indices are aggregated into a single national figure using demand-based weights. Weights reflect route importance in Indian domestic aviation.

Lead-time indices follow the same structure but group observations by booking window (T+1, T+7, etc.) to isolate the effect of advance purchase on fares.

05

Route Weights

The national index weights each route proportionally to its relative importance in India's domestic air travel demand.

Route City Pair Weight Share
DEL → BOM Delhi — Mumbai 0.35 35%
DEL → BLR Delhi — Bengaluru 0.25 25%
BOM → BLR Mumbai — Bengaluru 0.22 22%
DEL → HYD Delhi — Hyderabad 0.18 18%

Weights sum to 1.0 and are validated programmatically on every index computation. Route weight parameters are stored in data/route_weights.csv and not hardcoded into the computation logic.
