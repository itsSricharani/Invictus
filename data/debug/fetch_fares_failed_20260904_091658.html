    IndiGo BluChip - Loyalty Program

    IndiGo BluChip - Loyalty Program, as the name suggests, is a frequent flyer programme for IndiGo passengers. The idea behind it is simple: Earn more and fly more. This program is designed as point-based, where members can earn BluChip on flights and add-ons. These points are later redeemed for IndiGo flight tickets and other benefits. It is useful for frequent flyers looking to save on flight tickets.

    How does IndiGo BluChip work?

    To put it simply, see the steps below:

    1. Earn: You can book flights with IndiGo or use partnered services to earn points
    2. Accumulate: With every flight or service used, you earn points after completion
    3. Redeem: These earnings come as BluChip, which can be used to book flights

    Benefits and key features of IndiGo BluChip:

    • Earn BluChip: You can earn up to 16 BluChip per 100 INR spent on a flight
    • Earn on add-ons: Earn points on services like baggage, fast forward, and seat select
    • No blackout dates: You can redeem points on any IndiGo flights
    • Lifetime validity: BluChip does not expire as long as the account is active
    • Redemption: Use BluChip to book IndiGo flights
    • Nominee benefits: Redeem BluChip for up to 5 nominees
    • Codeshare earnings: You can also earn points on codeshare airlines.
